# engagement

1. Read `scope` and `rules_of_engagement`; stay inside both. Do not touch the
   platform or other teams.
2. Enumerate the estate: hosts, services, versions, credentials, shares.
3. For each finding record: asset, evidence, impact, reproduction, fix.
4. Save the report early and often with `PUT .../report`; it is revisable and
   the last version before the window closes is graded.
5. Write for a client: what you did, what you found, why it matters, how to fix.
6. Distinguish candidate, reproduced, and delivered findings; do not overclaim.
